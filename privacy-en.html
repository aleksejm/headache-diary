Headache Diary (Galvassāpju dienasgrāmata) – Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026 (updated 10 November 2026). Controller: AlMake, Latvia. Contact: almake.eng@gmail.com.

Summary

The app does not send your diary data to the internet. Everything you enter stays on your device only. The only network use is the optional Pro purchase, which is handled by Google Play (see below).

Data processed

You enter health-related data yourself: headache date and time, intensity, duration, symptoms, triggers, medication taken, and notes. Optionally, you can also enter profile details (name, gender, email, phone), mark menstruation days, and let the app record atmospheric pressure from the phone's barometer (if it has one; no permission is needed). This data also stays on your device only. This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.

What the app does not do

  • No account or registration.
  • No servers, no cloud storage.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No location or contacts permissions. No camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Pro purchase (version 1.7 and later)

Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. It is processed by Google Play using the Google Play Billing Library. For this reason the app contains the INTERNET and network-state permissions and the Google Play billing permission; they are used only to talk to Google Play about the purchase. Your payment details are handled by Google, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy. The app receives only the confirmation that Pro has been bought. Your diary entries are never sent with the purchase or anywhere else. The app's internal view blocks all network loading.

Screen lock

If you turn on the Pro PIN lock, only a salted hash of the PIN is stored on your device. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; the app never receives biometric data.

Export and sharing

You can create a PDF, CSV or JSON file and send it to your doctor or save it. The file is created on your device and you decide where it goes through the Android share sheet.

Deletion and backup

You can delete individual entries in the app; to delete all data, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Because nothing is stored in the cloud and Android automatic backup is disabled, we cannot restore your data. We recommend saving a JSON backup regularly.

Not a medical device

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It is a personal logbook only. Please consult a healthcare professional about your health.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

As we do not collect or store your data, it remains fully under your control. For questions, email the address above. Supervisory authority in Latvia: Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
